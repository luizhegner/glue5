# Code of conduct

GLUE 5 follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/):
in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else the project is
represented, be respectful and constructive, and leave out harassment,
personal attacks and discrimination of any kind.

The maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and other
contributions that break it, and may ban people who keep doing so.

To report a problem, use **Report content** on the comment and choose
**Report to repository admins**, or **Report abuse** on the user's
profile. Reports are handled confidentially.
